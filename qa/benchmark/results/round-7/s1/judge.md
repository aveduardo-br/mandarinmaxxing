### Verdict

A learner gets a clean, well-paced first sitting: correct pinyin, exemplary corrections, the right decisions about which rounds and clips to skip, and a solid first grammar pattern (我要 / 不要 / 吗). What they also get is a habit of stringing 你好, 对不起, 谢谢 and 再见 together as if any two made a sentence, in about a quarter of the Mandarin shown. The single change that would most improve it: in the under-ten-words rules, say that one speaker's line carries one reaction (greeting, thanks, apology, reply or farewell), with a second only where a real encounter joins them (好，再见 / 不客气，再见), and that the round, review or dictation is cut short rather than filled with pairs.

Within this run the same fault shows up in four separate places (Lesson 1 round 2, Lesson 1 dictation, Lesson 2 review, Lesson 2 round 3), which points at the instructions rather than chance: when words run out, the tutor fills the count by gluing reaction words instead of giving fewer prompts. Seen once: an incoherent round 3 prompt (T68); 对不起 used as "excuse me" (T74); a shared frame in round 2 (T54, T58).

Three changes I would make first:

1. **`SKILL.md`, "While the learner has met fewer than ten words".** Line to change: "A prompt can be one speaker's line or such an exchange, in the order of a real encounter: greet, then thank or apologize, then say goodbye." Add: "One speaker's line holds one reaction; a second only where a real encounter joins them (好，再见 / 不客气，再见). Never a greeting glued to thanks or an apology (你好，对不起 / 你好，谢谢), an apology glued to a goodbye (对不起，再见), 好 before a reply to an apology (好，没事), or two words that mean the same (没事，没关系)."

2. **`SKILL.md`, step 1 Review.** Line to change: "when the met words are too few for that many different prompts, one prompt can carry two or three of the words." This is what produced 您好，对不起！ and 你好，谢谢！. Change to: "…one prompt can carry two or three of the words as an exchange between two speakers, never as one speaker's string; if that still leaves fewer than five natural prompts, give fewer."

3. **`lesson-format.md`, Dictation.** Line to change: "With very few met words, two or three real lines are enough, and they may be lines the part already used." Add: "Each line must be natural alone: one reaction per line, 对不起 only as an apology for something that happened, never as 'excuse me' before a question or an offer." The same sentence about 对不起 belongs under "Natural" in `SKILL.md`'s "Every prompt".
