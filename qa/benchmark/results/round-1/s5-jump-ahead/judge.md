# Judge · round 1 · s5-jump-ahead

Scenario: the learner has finished Lesson 1 and asks for Lesson 22 (Getting sick, one part at pace 5: 病 看病 医生 生病 觉得 医院). They accept that the jump start's words count as known (Lessons 01 to 07) and that the route goes back to Lesson 2 afterwards. Met words: Lessons 01 to 07 plus Lesson 22. Lessons 08 to 21 are not met.

Standard used: `skills/mandarinmaxxing/SKILL.md` and `lesson-format.md`.

All three runs handle the scenario's main test well. In one message each tutor asks whether to count the jump start as known and confirms what comes after Lesson 22 (run 1 T2, run 2 T2, run 3 T2). None of the 45-odd prompts per run needs a word from Lessons 08 to 21, and the student never produced a `[NÃO SEI]` marker.

## Run 1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** The expected answers and corrections are natural. Problems:
- Narration (T50) `我觉得很高兴。` A native speaker would just say **我很高兴。** Using 觉得 with an emotion adjective here sounds like a translation.
- Narration (T50) `明天他想吃饭了。` Future 明天 combined with change-of-state 了 sounds odd for a plan. Fix: **他说明天想吃饭。** or drop 了: **明天他想吃饭。**
- Narration (T50) `今天我们去医院看病。` The speaker is taking the brother, not seeing a doctor themself. Acceptable in speech, though **今天我们带他去医院看病** is what people say. 带 is not met, so leaving it as is was reasonable.
- Round 2 · 4 (T32) `我觉得那个医院的医生很好。` Fine in speech, but **那家医院** is the standard measure word for a hospital.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is something a person would say. Early review lines are short exchanges, which the skill allows: T4 "— Obrigado, mamãe! — De nada.", T14 "Não foi nada, amigo. Você está bem hoje?"

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 swaps the slot of each of the four chunks (T16–T26). Round 3 has three connected sentences in four of five prompts (T36 "Ontem meu irmão mais novo ficou doente. Hoje ele vai ao hospital se consultar. O médico diz que ele está bem."). Round 2 slips twice. Round 2 · 3 (T30, "Meu pai está doente. Ele não quer comer.") and Round 2 · 5 (T34, "Amigo, você está bem? Você ficou doente?") have no when or where. They read more like round 3 or the review than a full action.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No prompt needs an unmet word, and the student gave no `[NÃO SEI]` markers. One borderline case: Round 2 · 4 expects 那个 (T32), which is its own entry in Lesson 14. Both of its characters are met (那 L05, 个 L07), so a learner can build it.

**5. Corrections: 4.** The translation corrections follow the format closely. Each has a blockquote, one line pointing at the fix with bold Hanzi and italic pinyin, then the full sentence on three lines: T8 (不有 → **没有**), T20 (word order **去医院看病**, given for a pinyin answer and shown in Hanzi), T26 (missing 了), T32 (喜欢 → **想**), T38 (time word position), T44 (吃水 → **喝**). Corrected targets come back: 没有 in T8, then Review 3; 吗 in T14, then Round 2 · 5. Pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T12, T28, T36), and the keyboard reminder comes once (T12). One lapse is in the dictation (T50). Sentence 4 left out 很, so it is a transcription miss, but the tutor treated it as "meaning right, more natural with 很", with no blockquote. The dictation format asks for the translation-correction format.

**6. Listening: 4.** The dictation has four separate sentences with the script hidden until the answer (T48–T50). The narration is first person, addresses no listener, quotes no one ("医生说他没事" is reported speech), and lasts about 20 seconds. Its questions sit in the same message, and the tutor marks each answer correct or incorrect (T52). Together the two clips use all six new words. The deductions are the two unnatural narration lines above.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** The steps come in order: review, vocabulary, three rounds, the choice of an extra round or listening (T46), listening, close. The tutor makes no comment on how the lesson is divided. The closing (T52) says what comes next: "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 2, parte 1." It offers no next part, although a single-part lesson of six words likely ran under the hour (see 8). The vocabulary table is Markdown rather than the HTML template, which may be a limit of the harness. Correctly, there is no role-play offer, since Lesson 22 has one part.

**8. Session length.** Six review prompts, the vocabulary, fifteen round prompts (round 3 with three sentences each), a four-line dictation and a five-question narration come to about 45–50 minutes. That is within reach of the hour target, but on the short side, so offering to start Lesson 2 part 1 would have been reasonable.

**Verdict.** A learner gets a clean, well-paced part. The prompts build from chunks to three-sentence accounts, the corrections are precise and come back, and nothing calls on Lessons 08 to 21. The weak spots are a few translated-sounding narration lines and a round 2 that sometimes forgets the when or where. The skill change that would help most is a concrete rule for when to offer the next part, because this part plainly ended early and the closing stayed silent.

## Run 2

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Most expected answers are natural. Problems:
- Round 3 · 5 (T48) `我觉得你没有病。` Correct, but **我觉得你没病。** is the everyday form.
- Review 8 (T18) `没事！今天我爸爸不在家。` Each half is natural, but together they don't connect ("It's nothing! Dad isn't home today."). The same goes for Review 6 (T16) `没事，我弟弟也在家。` Fix: give 没事 a reason to be said, e.g. **没事，我很好。**
- Round 3 · 1 (T40) `我觉得他明天去医院。` Acceptable, though 会去 would be more natural for a prediction.
- The dictation and narration (T52) are natural.

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** Most prompts are real sentences. Review 1 (T4) frames the exchange as a report: "Ele diz: 'Obrigado!' E eu respondo: 'De nada!'" That is closer to describing a scene than to saying the line. Reviews 6 and 8 join unrelated clauses only to fit 没事 in (see 1).

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 practices all four chunks (T20–T30). Round 2 is the strongest of the three runs: every prompt has who, when or where, and what (T30 "Hoje meu irmão mais novo está doente e não bebe chá", T36 "Acho que o médico também está no hospital hoje"). Round 3 never goes past two short sentences (T40, T42, T44, T46, T48), though jump start words would allow three. Run 1 shows that easily.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No prompt needs an unmet word, and the student gave no `[NÃO SEI]` markers.

**5. Corrections: 3.** The format is followed throughout (T10, T16, T22, T28, T34, T46 and dictation sentence 3 in T54), and pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T12, T20, T36, T44). Problems:
- T40 is partly wrong. The tutor says `他不要吃饭` "soa como uma ordem". As a third-person statement, 他不要吃饭 means "he doesn't want to eat" (要 = want, from L02). The prohibition reading belongs only to an imperative (不要吃饭！). 不想 is the softer, more common choice, but the reason given is false.
- T10 corrects word order (我水喝 → **喝水**), yet the follow-up prompt says "Vamos usar **没关系** de novo" (T16). 没关系 was never the error. Review 7 happens to bring back 喝水, but it re-tests the wrong target by name.
- The review counter drifts: "Revisão 6 de 6" (T14), then "7 de 7" (T16), then "8 de 8" (T18). The format wants the counter to count the whole review.

**6. Listening: 4.** The dictation format is correct, and the missed 都 is corrected with a blockquote (T54). The narration (T54) is first person, has no quotes, uses 生病 and 看病, and runs about 20 seconds. The questions come with the clip, and the tutor marks each answer (T56). Missing: the narration script is never shown after the learner answers. The script is hidden only until the answer, and run 1 and run 3 both reveal it afterwards.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** The steps come in order, the extra round or listening is offered (T50), and there is no comment on how the lesson is divided. T4 confirms the route ("a rota fica 22, 2 a 21"). The closing (T56) names Lesson 2, part 1, but offers no next part. The table is Markdown.

**8. Session length.** Eight review prompts, fifteen short round prompts, a dictation and a narration come to about 40–45 minutes. That is under the hour, so the next part should have been offered.

**Verdict.** A learner gets solid practice with clear when-and-where sentences in round 2. However, round 3 stays at two short lines, so the part feels thinner than the met words allow. The learner also takes away one false grammar rule (不要 "sounds like an order") and one muddled re-test. The skill change that would help most is telling the tutor to rework a planned prompt for a re-ask, as the rounds already do, rather than appending one. That keeps the review counter fixed and ties the re-ask to the item that actually went wrong.

## Run 3

**1. Natural Mandarin: 3.** One collocation keeps coming back and sounds off: `病没事` ("the illness is nothing").
- Round 1 · 4 (T24) `医生说爸爸的病没事。` → **医生说爸爸没事。** or **医生说爸爸的病没什么。** (什么 is met)
- Round 2 · 4 (T34) `我觉得我的病没事。` → **我觉得我没事。**
- Round 3 · 3 (T42) `医生说弟弟的病没事。` → **医生说弟弟没事。**
- Narration (T52) `医生说我的病没事。` → **医生说我没事。**

Also, Round 2 · 2 (T30) `这个医院的医生很好` is fine in speech, but **这家医院** is standard. The rest is natural: `我觉得我生病了`, `她没有生病，她是医生`, `今天我和妈妈去医院看病`.

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** Four of the seven review prompts are reported dialogue: "Meu amigo disse: 'Obrigado!' Eu respondi: 'De nada!'" (T4). The same shape appears in T8, T14 and T16. They are grammatical, but they describe an exchange instead of being the line someone says. The round prompts are all real sentences or messages (T40 "Oi, amigo! Hoje eu estou doente, não vou. Desculpa!").

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 covers all four chunks (T18–T28). In round 2, most prompts have a when or where (T28 "Amanhã meu pai e eu vamos ao hospital…", T32 "Hoje minha mãe…"), but Round 2 · 4 has no time. Round 3 is mostly two or three connected sentences (T42, T46), with a good mix of an account, a message and a plan.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No prompt needs an unmet word, and the student gave no `[NÃO SEI]` markers. One borderline case: 这个 (a Lesson 14 entry, built from met 这 and 个) in Round 2 · 2.

**5. Corrections: 4.** The translation corrections are precise and follow the format: T10 (不客气 → **没关系**, with the reason), T22 and T38 (word order; T38 links back: "É a mesma ordem de antes"), T30 (missing **和**, explaining the change of meaning), T46 (missing **是**). Corrected items come back and are acknowledged (T40 "Agora a ordem e o **和** saíram certos"). Pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T12, T20, T28, T36, T44). Lapses: T16 handles 我好 as unnatural phrasing but leaves out the meaning line of the three-line pattern. Dictation sentence 4 (T52) gives the fix line but no full corrected sentence before the script. The review counter changes from "de 6" to "de 7" after a re-ask (T10).

**6. Listening: 5.** The dictation has four sentences and stays hidden until the answer (T50–T52). The narration is labeled by scene, and its questions come before the clip. It is first person, addresses no listener, quotes no one, runs 20–25 seconds, and the script is revealed with pinyin and translation after the answers (T54). Together the two clips use all six new words. The tutor checked comprehension well: it caught the student's "amigo do pai" error. The only blemish is the `病没事` line, already counted under 1.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** The steps come in order, with the extra round or listening offered (T48). T18 "Primeiro, uma revisão da última parte que você estudou" is a harmless signpost, not a comment on how the lesson is divided. The closing (T54) names Lesson 2, part 1, but offers no next part. The table is Markdown.

**8. Session length.** Seven review prompts, fifteen round prompts and two clips come to about 45–50 minutes. That fits roughly, but leaves room for the offer to continue.

**Verdict.** This is the most varied part of the three. Round 3 mixes accounts, messages and plans, the corrections are explained and followed up, and the listening is model-quality. A learner would, however, absorb `X的病没事` as a set phrase, since it appears four times, and the review leans on stiff "he said / I replied" frames. The skill change that would help most is a line telling the tutor to write the line itself, not a report of it, in review and round prompts.

## Cross-run summary

**Problems in more than one run (these point at the instructions):**
- **No offer of the next part.** All three runs end at about 40–50 minutes on a single-part, six-word lesson, and none offers to start Lesson 2 part 1. The skill names "the jump start and at pace 3" as the cases that end early, so tutors don't see a short single-part lesson as one.
- **Re-asks in the review push the counter up.** Run 2 (6 → 7 → 8) and run 3 (6 → 7). The format fixes "Review 2 of 7" but says nothing about re-asks. The rounds already have a rule ("keep each round at five by reworking a prompt still to come"); the review does not.
- **Reported-dialogue frames in the review.** Run 2 (T4) and run 3 (T4, T8, T14, T16) write "X said: '…' I replied: '…'" to fit a one-word reply into a "full sentence longer than the chunk". This is the review rule ("longer than the chunk it tests") pushing against the whole-sentence rule.
- **这个/那个医院.** Runs 1 and 3 expect 这个 or 那个, which are separate Lesson 14 entries built from met characters. Not a violation, but the skill doesn't say whether such compounds count as met, and 家 is the natural measure word anyway.
- **Dictation slips handled outside the correction format.** Run 1 (T50, treated as "unnatural") and run 3 (T52, no full sentence) loosen the format when the error is a missed word in a dictation.
- **Markdown table instead of the HTML template** in all three. This is probably the harness, but worth checking.

**Problems seen once:**
- Run 2: a false explanation of 不要 (T40), a re-ask aimed at the wrong item (T16), round 3 kept to two short lines, and the narration script never revealed.
- Run 3: the recurring unnatural `病没事` (four times).
- Run 1: round 2 prompts without a when or where (T30, T34), and narration lines `我觉得很高兴` and `明天他想吃饭了`.

**Three changes to make first:**
1. `SKILL.md`, the session paragraph: "If a part ends well before that, as happens in the jump start and at pace 3, offer in the closing line to start the next part now." Change it to something like: "If a part ends well before that, as happens in the jump start, at pace 3, and in any part of six words or fewer, offer in the closing line to start the next part now. When in doubt, offer." All three runs missed this.
2. `lesson-format.md`, Prompts: "In the review the counter counts the whole review, such as **Review 2 of 7**." Add: "Keep the total you announced. When a correction calls for a re-ask, rework a review prompt still to come so it uses the corrected item (the item that went wrong, not the prompt's original target), as in the rounds; add one only if the review would otherwise drop below five." This fixes the counter drift (runs 2 and 3) and the misaimed re-ask (run 2).
3. `SKILL.md`, review and rounds: "Every prompt is a whole sentence someone would actually say or write, in the learner's language, never a fragment or a description of a scene." Add: "Write the line itself, not a report of it: avoid frames like 'He said: "…" I replied: "…"'. To make a review prompt longer than its chunk, add a clause the speaker would say, such as a reason or a time." This applies to review 1 in run 2 and four review prompts in run 3.

Also worth adding (lower priority): one line in `SKILL.md` saying a map entry made of met characters (这个, 那个) counts as met only once its own block is taught, or naming 家 as the measure word for places.
