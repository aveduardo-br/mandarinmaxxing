## Run 1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Most expected answers are natural (T2 现在三点半，我弟弟在家; T22 今天三月五号; R2.3 明天上午你几点起床？). Unnatural or weak lines:
- R3.3 (T42) 今天他们很早起床，六点。 The time tacked on after a comma reads as an afterthought. Fix: 今天他们六点起床，很早。 Same pattern in R3.4 (T46) 我哥哥很晚起床，下午两点。 → 我哥哥下午两点起床，很晚。 and in the narration (T52) 我妈妈很早起床，五点半。 → 我妈妈五点半起床，很早。
- R2.2 (T30) 今天下午我两个孩子在家。 Acceptable colloquially; more natural: 今天下午我的两个孩子都在家。
- Rev 4 (T8) 没关系，你们现在想喝茶吗？ 没关系 with nothing to forgive is a stretch ("Não tem problema!" as an opener). Better: a prompt that answers an apology, e.g. 对不起！— 没关系。
- The correction at T26 rejects a natural sentence (see 5).

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** All prompts are sayable sentences. Rev 2 (T4) "Que horas são agora? Nós vamos à casa de um amigo." and Rev 4 join loosely related clauses to fit 几点/没关系. R3.2 (T40) "Pai, agora são cinco e meia. Quando você está em casa? Eu e meu irmão mais novo estamos em casa." is a slightly odd message but plausible.

**3. Round progression: 5.** Round 1 swaps each chunk's slot (T18 我七点起床, T20 你弟弟什么时候起床, T24 我很晚起床). Round 2 gives full actions with who + when + what (T28 "Meu pai acorda às cinco e meia, muito cedo"). Round 3 has two- and three-sentence accounts and a message (T38 "Hoje é domingo. Eu acordo às nove da manhã. À tarde, eu vou à casa de um amigo."). Every new word appears in at least three round prompts.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]` markers; my reading finds no unmet word. Older words: 月/号/星期天 (line 3), 姐姐/弟弟 (line 7), 我们/你们/他们 (line 14); 几点 and 没关系 both woven in.

**5. Corrections: 3.** Format is right throughout (blockquote, one bolded fix with pinyin, full sentence once with hard line breaks), and pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T10, T18, T34, T42). The first-pinyin keyboard reminder is at T10. Good targeted fixes: T8 month before day, T20 点 vs 号, T32 两个孩子, T38 和 cannot join clauses, T44 只 → 个 "Só a primeira frase muda". But **T26 is wrong**: the learner wrote *Wǒ qǐchuáng hěn wǎn* (我起床很晚), which is grammatical, natural Mandarin; the tutor marked it incorrect ("很晚 vem antes do verbo"). It should have been accepted, at most noting the taught chunk.

**6. Listening: 5.** Dictation of four sentences from the part's words, corrected in format, script then shown with pinyin and translation (T52). Narration (T52) is first person, seven sentences (~25 s), no listener addressed, no quotes; questions sent with the clip; answers marked one by one (T54). All six new words appear across the step (时候 via 什么时候 in dictation).

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** Review → vocabulary → three rounds → offer of extra round or listening (T48) → listening → close. Title once above the review and repeated above the table (Markdown fallback), "Traduza para o mandarim" only at the two allowed places. Closing "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 11, parte 3." with no comment on the division. Issues: the closing line is appended to the narration feedback rather than sent as its own short message; the saved **To reinforce** was not pruned (it keeps 三月五号, 我姐姐九点半给我打电话 and 我七点起床, all later answered correctly without help, and adds 我很晚起床 from the wrong correction). The 只/个 slip in round 3 is saved but could not be revisited.

**8. Session length: 4.** About 27 exchanges: 8 review prompts (~8 min), vocabulary (~4 min), round 1 (~5), round 2 (~8), round 3 (~12), dictation and narration (~10): about 45 minutes. Close enough to the hour that no next-part offer was required.

**Verdict.** A learner gets a well-shaped, fully met-word lesson with clear progression and good, short corrections, and leaves able to say when people get up and what they do in the morning and afternoon. The one real harm is being told that a correct sentence (我起床很晚) is wrong, which teaches a false rule. The single change that would most improve it: tell the tutor in `lesson-format.md` that any word order a native speaker accepts is correct even when it differs from the taught chunk.

## Run 2

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Good, idiomatic choices: 早 taught as the morning greeting (妈妈，早！), 你今天几点吃饭？, 明天下午两点半我和弟弟在家. Unnatural or weak lines:
- Narration (T54) 我下午三点半去，我不想晚。 "我不想晚" is not idiomatic. Fix: 我下午三点半去，不想去晚了。 (or 我不想迟到, but 迟到 is unmet).
- Chunk (T20) 对不起，我晚了。 and R1.4 (T26) 对不起，我哥哥晚了。 Native apology is 对不起，我来晚了 (来 unmet). The chunk is tolerable; apologizing with 我哥哥晚了 is weaker: 对不起，我哥哥来晚了.
- R3.1 (T40) 我起床，想喝茶。 → 我起床了，想喝茶。 (了 is met).
- The correction at T34 rejects a natural sentence (see 5).

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is something a person would say: T2 "Irmãozinho, que horas são agora?", T26 "Bom dia, mamãe! Você quer chá?", T42 "Mamãe, desculpe, eu estou atrasado. Quando vocês vão comer?" Minor: Rev 4 (T8) says "Meu irmão" without older/younger while expecting 哥哥.

**3. Round progression: 5.** Round 1 swaps each chunk (什么时候起床, 上午九点起床 → 下午三点吃饭, 我晚了 → 我哥哥晚了, 早). Round 2 full actions (T30 "Amanhã, às dez da manhã, eu vou à casa do meu amigo."). **4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]`; none unmet by my reading. Older words: 月/号/星期天 (line 3), 弟弟/爱 (line 7), 他们/她们/你们/我们 (line 14); 几点 and 没关系 woven in.

**5. Corrections: 3.** Format correct with hard line breaks; pinyin answers confirmed with Hanzi (T10, T18, T26, T42, T50); keyboard reminder at T10. Every correction is brought back later as the skill asks (吗 at Rev 6, 几点吃饭 at Rev 9, 晚了 at R2.4, 两点 at R3.5, 和他吃饭 at R3.4). Precise fixes: T22 什么时候 kept together, T28 晚了, T40 二点 → 两点, T46 和他们吃饭. But **T34 is wrong**: *Jīntiān wǒ gēge qǐchuáng hěn zǎo* (今天我哥哥起床很早) is acceptable Mandarin and was marked incorrect. T46 shows only the corrected second sentence instead of the full answer once (minor).

**6. Listening: 5.** Five-sentence dictation using all six new words (T52), corrected and then shown with pinyin and translation (T54). Narration first person, seven clauses (~25 s), no listener addressed; five questions in the same message; answers marked (T56).

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** All five steps in order, extra-round offer at T50, closing "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 11, parte 3." with no commentary. **To reinforce** saved correctly: every earlier slip was later answered right, so only the dictation's 下午三点半 remains. Closing is appended to the narration feedback (minor).

**8. Session length: 4.** About 28 exchanges with 9 review prompts and longer round 3 prompts: about 50 minutes. Fits the target.

**Verdict.** The strongest of the three: natural prompts, the greeting 早 and the "I'm late" chunk give the learner real everyday lines, and every correction is recycled. The learner gets a solid, usable part, but hears one unnatural audio line (我不想晚) and is told a correct word order (起床很早) is wrong. The single change: the same rule as run 1, so that natural alternatives to a chunk are accepted, not corrected.

## Run 3

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Strong chunk choice 起床的时候 / 吃饭的时候 (T14), natural sentences such as 我吃饭的时候喝水, 我姐姐打电话的时候，我在家, 他不在家，他在中国. Unnatural or wrong:
- Rev 4 (T8) 对不起，现在两点半。没关系！ One speaker apologizing and then saying "no problem" is incoherent. Fix: two speakers, or 对不起！— 没关系，现在才两点半。 (才 unmet; simpler: a prompt replying to an apology).
- Narration (T48) 我很晚起床，十点半。 afterthought time. Fix: 我十点半起床，很晚。 Same in R3.3 (T38) 昨天我女儿很晚起床，九点半。 → 昨天我女儿九点半起床，很晚。
- Usage note (T14) "时候 vem depois de 的" is inaccurate: 什么时候 has no 的. Fix: "时候 também aparece em ...的时候 (quando ...)".
- Rev 3 (T6) 我妈妈爱家人，五点吃饭。 grammatical but an odd pairing made to fit 爱/家人.

**2. Whole sentences: 3.** Rev 4 is not something a person would say (see above), and Rev 3 ("Minha mãe ama a família e come às cinco horas") is two unrelated facts glued together to carry older words. The rest are good (T36 "Amigo, agora são três e meia da tarde. Você está em casa? Eu ligo para você.").

**3. Round progression: 5.** Round 1 practices the chunks (你几点起床, 下午三点, 吃饭的时候, 很早/很晚起床); round 2 full actions with who, when, what (T24 "Amanhã às nove da manhã eu ligo para a minha mãe."); round 3 three-sentence accounts (T40 "Meu irmão mais novo tem onze anos este ano. Ele levanta às sete, bem cedo. No domingo ele levanta muito tarde."). Each new word in three or more prompts. The round 1 label says "Blocos" rather than a chunk name (cosmetic).

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]`; none unmet by my reading. Older words: 月/号/星期天 (line 3), 爱/家人/姐姐 (line 7), 我们/都 (line 14); 几点 and 没关系 woven in.

**5. Corrections: 4.** Each correction targets only the slip and is correct: T8 time before verb, T24 弟弟 vs 哥哥, T30 不在家, T36 很晚, T42 早 vs 晚; pinyin answers confirmed with Hanzi (T10, T26, T34); keyboard reminder at T10. Problems: no hard line breaks anywhere, so the Hanzi / pinyin / meaning lines would render as one run-on paragraph, against "Use hard line breaks so the three lines don't run together". T18 rejects 打你电话, which is common spoken mainland usage; steering to 给你打电话 is fine but could be framed as the standard form, not an error. 不在家 (T30) is not brought back in round 3.

**6. Listening: 4.** Five-sentence dictation covering all six new words; narration first person, eight short sentences (~25–30 s), no listener addressed, questions sent with the clip, answers marked (T50). But after the dictation, "O texto completo do ditado" (T48) lists only four of the five sentences (sentence 3 is missing), and question 4 bundles two questions.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** Steps in order, extra-round offer at T44, closing "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 11, parte 3." with no commentary. The review counter changes mid-review: "Revisão 1 de 5" … "3 de 5", then "Revisão 4 de 6" after adding a retest; the total should be fixed at the start. **To reinforce** saved as 不在家 去朋友家, which is consistent with what was missed and later fixed.

**8. Session length: 4.** 6 review prompts, 15 round prompts, two listening clips: about 45 minutes. Acceptable for the hour.

**Verdict.** The learner gets the most useful extra structure of the three runs (…的时候 "when …") and corrections that are all substantively right, so they leave able to narrate a morning with times and "when" clauses. The review is the weak spot: two forced prompts built to carry older words, and a counter that changes. The single change: in `SKILL.md`, weave older words and items to reinforce only into sentences that make sense as one situation, and give them their own prompt otherwise.

## Cross-run summary

**Problems in more than one run (point at the instructions):**
- **Natural alternatives marked wrong.** Runs 1 (T26) and 2 (T34) both reject 起床很晚/起床很早 because the taught chunk was 很早起床. The rule "Accept any natural version that meets the request" exists but is stated only under "Correct and natural", and the tutor does not apply it to word order.
- **Time tacked on after a comma** (…起床，六点 / 很晚起床，十点半). Seen in runs 1 and 3, in expected answers and narration scripts. The script check in `lesson-format.md` covers only listening scripts, not expected answers or chunks.
- **Older words and reinforce items forced into unrelated clauses** in the review: run 1 Rev 2 and Rev 4, run 3 Rev 3 and Rev 4.
- **Closing appended to the narration feedback** instead of a separate short message (all three). Harmless, but `lesson-format.md` says "Send one short closing message after the last exercise".
- Shared strengths: all three runs used only met words, honored lines 3/7/14 and the reinforce items, kept the three-round progression, and gave correct first-pinyin reminders.

**Problems seen once:** run 1 did not prune **To reinforce** (keeps items later answered correctly); run 2's unnatural narration line 我不想晚; run 3's missing hard line breaks, the changing review total, the inaccurate 时候 usage note, and the incomplete dictation script.

**Three changes I would make first:**
1. `lesson-format.md`, under "Respond to a written translation": change "**Correct and natural.** Confirm briefly and move to the next prompt. Accept any natural version that meets the request." to add: "A different word order or construction that a native speaker would accept is correct, even when it is not the chunk you taught (我起床很早 and 我很早起床 are both right). Never mark it incorrect; at most show the taught chunk in one line."
2. `SKILL.md`, in "Teach one part at a time": after "Every prompt is a whole sentence someone would actually say or write…", add: "Write the expected Mandarin answer before the prompt and check it as you would a listening script: one natural clause where possible, time words before the verb (我六点起床，很早, not 我很早起床，六点)." Also extend `lesson-format.md`'s "**Review the script.** Read it through: the Mandarin sounds natural" so it applies to chunks and expected answers as well.
3. `SKILL.md`, Review step: change "Weave the saved items to reinforce and the older words (below) into these same sentences; add prompts for them only while the total stays at ten or fewer." to "Weave the saved items to reinforce and the older words (below) into these same sentences only where they fit one natural situation; otherwise give them a prompt of their own while the total stays at ten or fewer. Fix the review total in the first counter and keep retests inside it."
