# Run notes

Scenario: s2-rotation (run-1). Turns: 54 (27 student, 27 tutor).

Unclear points in SKILL.md / lesson-format.md:

1. Review size vs. the previous part. The previous part (1-11.1) had 3 words, but the review needs "at least five and at most ten". "One prompt per new word of the previous part in the Done log, at least five and at most ten." I filled the gap with the 2 reinforce items and 6 older words, and used 8 prompts, 2 of them re-asks after errors. It is not clear whether the planned total should include slots reserved for re-asks, so the counter "of 8" was fixed from the start.
2. Older words from a line with more than five words. "A line with more than five words gives two words per visit." Lines 3, 7 and 14 back each had 6 words, so 2 words each gave exactly 6 (the maximum). I took this as the first visit to each line at that distance. The skill does not say how to know which visit it is when a line was never visited at that distance, so I assumed the 1st and 2nd words (月 号, 姐姐 弟弟, 我们 你们).
3. To reinforce update. "remove the items the learner used correctly without help during that part, then add the new ones". Unclear whether an item the learner missed and then answered correctly on the re-ask in the same part is added or not. I added every item that needed a correction (8 items, under the cap of 10), and removed 几点 and 没关系, which were answered right. I used commas between items because chunks contain spaces and the old line used spaces.
4. Close. "Send one short closing message after the last exercise." The last exercise's feedback and the closing were put in one tutor turn to keep strict alternation (the student has nothing to reply to). Unsure if it should be a separate message. I left out the offer to start the next part because the part used a full hour of exercises.
5. Review title. "When a part starts with review, the title line opens the review." I put the title line at the top of T2, and the table heading repeats it. Counters in rounds ("Bateria 1 · 1 de 5") come right under the round name line ("Bateria 1 · Frases"), which looks redundant but follows the quoted format: "Open each round with one bold line that names it... counter... such as Round 2 · 3 of 5".
6. Dictation label. The Labels line has no word for "dictation" or "narration" (Ditado, Áudio), so I used "Ditado 1" and "Áudio 1 · ..." as in the format examples.
7. Chunk 上午九点 · 下午三点 is two phrases in one chunk (slot: the hour and the part of the day); the skill allows two to four chunks but does not say whether a chunk may hold two variants.
8. Markdown fallback table: no HTML widget, so used a plain Markdown table with bold Hanzi, since the skill says to use bold Hanzi when only standard formatting is confirmed.
9. 星期四 was used in a prompt: the Done log has 星期 and the numbers, not 星期四 as a word. I treated it as built from known words.
