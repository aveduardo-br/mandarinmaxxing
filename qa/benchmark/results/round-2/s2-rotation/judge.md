## Run 1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Most expected answers are clean, mainland Mandarin with time words placed correctly (T8 我两点半给妈妈打电话; T32 现在很晚了，我不想喝茶). Unnatural or weak sentences:
- T39/T40 `今天我很晚起床。` is used for a single past event ("Hoje eu acordei muito tarde"). Without an aspect marker it reads as a habit. A native would say **今天我起晚了**, but that resultative isn't taught yet. A fix with met words is **今天我十点起床，很晚。**, or rewriting the prompt as a habit.
- T41/T42 `我弟弟很晚起床，上午十点。` The time tacked on as an afterthought is awkward. Fix: **我弟弟上午十点起床，很晚。**
- T48 narration: `上午去朋友的家` is understandable, but **上午去朋友家** is what people say. `我上午十点起床，很晚。` is acceptable.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is a line someone would say. The review exchange at T10 ("Desculpe, senhor! — Não foi nada!") is permitted in the dash format and keeps the order of a real encounter.

**3. Round progression: 5.** Round 1 swaps the slots of the four chunks (T14 你几点起床, T16 明天上午十点, T18 什么时候, T22 很早 / T24 很晚). Round 2 gives complete actions with who, when and what (T26 今天下午给哥哥打电话; T32 星期天我上午八点半起床), though T24 "Meu pai e minha mãe acordam muito cedo" is thin on when or where. Round 3 has three-sentence sequences throughout (T34, T38, T42). Every new word appears in at least three prompts.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]` markers. By my reading, 0 of 21 prompts need an unmet word. "Já é tarde" (T30) avoids 已经 by expecting 现在很晚了. All three older words due by turn are used in the review itself: 弟弟 (T8), 们 in 他们 (T12), 月 in 三月 (T12).

**5. **6. Listening: 5.** The dictation (T46) has five single-turn sentences from one speaker. The narration (T48) is first person, seven sentences, a concrete Sunday, with no addressee and no quotes. Its four questions are sent in the same message as the clip, and the script appears only after the answers. Across the two clips all six new words appear (上午 only in the narration).

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** The part runs review → vocabulary → three rounds → offer of another round or listening (T44) → dictation and narration → close. The closing (T50) reads "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 11, parte 3. Quer começar agora?" **8. Session length.** 24 learner replies, six of them multi-sentence, plus a six-word table and two clips: about 45 to 55 minutes. That fits the hour, and the extra round was offered.

**Verdict.** This is the strongest run. A learner gets a well-paced part, each new word in four or five varied prompts, accurate and minimal corrections, a clean listening step, and the older words brought back exactly by turn. The only blemishes are a couple of sentences where 很晚/很早 + 起床 is used for a single past event or with a tacked-on time. The skill change that would help most is a rule that, when the natural Mandarin needs a pattern the learner hasn't met (得 complements, 才), the prompt gets rewritten instead of accepting a near-miss.

## Run 2

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** The expected answers in the review and rounds are natural (T26 在家喝茶; T40 我的朋友们都在家). The problems are in the narration (T46):
- `我在家喝茶，也吃饭。` "也吃饭" tacked on is stilted. Fix: **我在家喝茶、吃饭。** or **我在家吃饭，喝茶。**
- `她妹妹很晚起床，现在也在家。` "现在" in a past account of the morning clashes with the timeline. Fix: **她妹妹很晚起床，也在家。**
- `明天我也去她家。` 也 implies someone else is going too. Fix: **明天我还去她家** (还 not met), or simply **明天我去她家。**
- `我去中国朋友的家` is ambiguous ("to China, a friend's home" / "a Chinese friend's home"). Fix: **我去朋友家。我朋友是中国人。**

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** The review prompts are bare. "São nove horas." (T4) and "São seis e meia." (T6) are sentences, but they practice only clock reading. "Não tem problema!" (T10) is a reaction with nothing to react to. The skill asks for a context that fits only 没关系 or 没事, or for accepting both. Both were accepted, but the prompt has no situation. The rounds are fine.

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 is good, each prompt swapping a chunk slot (T14–T22). Round 2 mostly gives full actions (T26 who, when, where and what), but T32 "Minha irmã mais velha se levanta muito tarde" has no when or where. Round 3 has proper two- and three-sentence sequences (T34–T42). The review is the weak point: five near-drill prompts with no older words, although the previous part had only three words and the skill says to fill the review with reinforce items *and* older words. The older words 弟弟 (T24), 们 (T28, T40) and 月 (T34) do appear in the rounds, which the skill allows.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]` markers, and 0 of 20 prompts need an unmet word.

**5. Corrections: 4.** The structure is right (blockquote, fix line, full sentence), but several fix lines leave out the pinyin the format requires: T14 "A hora vem antes do verbo: **六点半起床**." and T20 "**明天很晚起床**". In the dictation feedback (T46), sentence 4 gets a full corrected sentence and then appears again in the script right below it. The content of every correction is right (T32 姐姐/妹妹; T26 在家 before the verb). The keyboard reminder appears once (T10).

**6. Listening: 4.** The dictation (T44) is good: five single-speaker sentences that cover 早, 上午, 时候, 下午 and 晚. The narration is first person with no addressee, and its five questions include a chunk-in-context question (很晚起床). However, it names the friend `小王`: the learner has never met 王, so the name is unknown sound in a listening clip. It also has the tense slip and the stray 也 noted above.

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** The five steps come in order, the extra round is offered at T42, and the closing (T48) gives the next part and the offer to start now, with no commentary.

**8. Session length.** 22 learner replies, the review very light, plus listening: about 40 to 45 minutes. That fits, a little short.

**Verdict.** A learner gets solid rounds and a usable dictation, but the review is thin and drill-like, and the narration is the clunkiest of the three runs. Its Mandarin is grammatical but not how a native would tell the story, and it includes an unmet name. The skill change that would help most is to make the narration check concrete: no proper names the learner hasn't met, and tense and 也/现在 coherence checked like a dictation sentence. This run also shows that the review-filling rule for parts with fewer than five words needs to say that the older words go into the review.

## Run 3

**1. Natural Mandarin: 3.** The tutor teaches `我起床很早。` as a chunk (T14) and builds on it throughout: T22 我哥哥起床很晚, T36 我弟弟起床很早, T40, and dictation sentence 1. Verb + bare 很 + adjective after 起床 is a non-standard calque. Natives say **我起得很早** (得 not met) or **我很早起床** / **我七点起床，很早**, which the learner can already say. List:
- T14 chunk `我起床很早。` Fix: **我很早起床。**
- T22 `我哥哥起床很晚。` Fix: **我哥哥很晚起床。**
- T36 `我弟弟起床很早。` Fix: **我弟弟很早起床。**
- T40 `我哥哥起床很晚，上午十点。我起床很早，六点。` Fix: **我哥哥上午十点起床，很晚。我六点起床，很早。**
- T42 `对不起，现在几点？` uses 对不起 for "excuse me" when asking a question, which is an apology, not an opener. Fix: **请问，现在几点？** (请问 is met).
- T46 dictation 1 `我哥哥起床很晚。` Fix as above.

The other sentences are fine (T34 现在十一点，很晚了; T38 我爸爸也去).

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** All prompts are real lines. The review dash exchange (T8) is permitted.

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 drifts: T20 "Agora são nove horas da manhã" and T24 "Agora são três e meia da tarde" are the same frame twice (现在 + period of day + time), which practices one chunk and none of the action chunks. Round 2 gives full actions (T28 他们明天上午都去中国; T30), though T34 "Agora são onze horas, já é tarde" is a state, not an action. Round 3 has good three-sentence sequences (T36, T42, T44).

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]` markers, and 0 of 21 prompts need an unmet word. All three older words due by turn are in the review: 弟弟 (T6), 月 (T12), 们 (T14).

**5. Corrections: 5.** Every correction has the blockquote, one fix line in bold with pinyin and a short reason, and the full sentence once (T8, T14, T20, T26, T32, T38, T44). The pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T10, T18, T34, T42). The keyboard reminder appears once (T10). The corrected items come back later: 们 at T28, 上午 order at T36 and T40, 喝茶 at T44, 也 at T42. In the dictation (T48), the corrected sentence is shown and then repeated in the script.

**6. Listening: 4.** The dictation (T46) has four single-turn sentences. The narration (T48) is first person, seven sentences, with no addressee, and all six new words appear across the two clips. However, it asks only three questions and leaves out what the speaker wants or plans (the script ends with 我也想去中国, an obvious target) and any chunk-in-context question.

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** The steps come in the right order, the extra round is offered (T44), and the closing (T50) has the next part and the offer to start now, with no commentary.

**8. Session length.** 24 learner replies, a four-sentence dictation and a three-question narration: about 40 to 45 minutes. That fits.

**Verdict.** Mechanically this run is very tidy: exact rotation of older words, clean corrections, correct flow. But a learner would leave having drilled `起床很早/很晚` as a set phrase, which a Chinese teacher would correct. The skill change that would help most is to require that the chunks themselves pass the naturalness check, and that a chunk whose natural form needs an unmet pattern (here 得) be rebuilt from forms the learner can already use (很早起床, 七点起床，很早).

## Cross-run summary

**Problems in more than one run (point at the instructions):**
- **很早/很晚 with 起床 handled awkwardly.** Run 1 uses 很晚起床 for a single past event and tacks on the time ("很晚起床，上午十点"). Run 3 builds a non-standard 起床很早 chunk and uses the same tacked-on time. The natural form (起得很早) needs 得, which isn't met, and no instruction tells the tutor what to do when the natural form is out of reach. Run 2's pattern (五点起床，很早) shows the safe answer.
- **The dictation correction duplicates the script.** In runs 2 and 3, each wrong sentence gets the full corrected sentence and then the full script repeats it. `lesson-format.md` asks for both.
- **Thin or repetitive clock-time prompts.** Run 2's review and run 3's round 1 spend prompts on bare "São X horas" frames, a sign that the instructions don't say what to do when the previous part's words are all clock words.
- **Narration questions vary** from three (run 3) to five (run 2), and only one run covers all three kinds the format names.

**Problems seen only once:**
- Run 2: an unmet name (小王) and tense/也 slips in the narration, fix lines without pinyin, and older words left out of a review that had too few words of its own.
- Run 3: 对不起 used for "excuse me" before a question (it should be 请问).
- Run 1: a meaning line that doesn't match the prompt's tense (T40).

**The three changes I would make first:**
1. `SKILL.md`, under "Teach one part at a time": *"Before sending a prompt, have its Mandarin answer ready and check it as you would a listening script: natural, mainland usage, time words before the verb, only met words."* Add: *"Check the chunks the same way. If the natural sentence needs a word or pattern the learner hasn't met (a 得 complement, 才, 已经), change the prompt or the chunk, for example 七点起床，很早, never a near-miss such as 起床很早."*
2. `lesson-format.md`, Dictation: *"Correct each sentence with the written-translation format, then show the script."* Change to: *"For each wrong sentence, give the blockquote and the fix line only, then show the full script once with pinyin and meaning; the script stands for the corrected sentences."*
3. `lesson-format.md`, Narration: *"…with the questions in the same message so the learner can read them before playing: what happened, what the speaker wanted or plans, what a chunk meant in context."* Change to: *"…three to five questions, at least one of each kind: what happened, what the speaker wants or plans, and what a chunk of the part meant in context. Use no names the learner hasn't met."* (A close fourth is `SKILL.md`, Review: *"fill the review with the items to reinforce and the older words (below), each in a prompt of its own"*. Add *"put the older words picked for this part in the review itself"* so run 2's drill-only review can't happen.)
