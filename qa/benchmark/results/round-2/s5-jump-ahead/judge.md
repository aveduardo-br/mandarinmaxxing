## run-1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Nearly every expected answer is natural mainland Mandarin. Minor issues:
- T48 narration: 我没有大病，妈妈很高兴。 The two clauses sit side by side with no link, and 没有大病 is a little bare. Fix: **我没什么大病，所以妈妈很高兴。** If 所以 counts as unmet, use **我没什么大病。妈妈很高兴。**
- T12 Review 5: the expected 好，妈妈，没关系。 strings two reassurances together, which is contrived. Fix: use one item per prompt, for example a real trigger and then **没关系**.
- Not a language error, but it matters: T10 rejects 没有关系, which is standard Mandarin (see dimension 5).

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** Every prompt is a line someone could say. But Review 3, 5 and 6 (T8 "Tudo bem, não tem problema.", T12 "Está bem, mamãe, não tem problema.", T14 "Não foi nada!") are three bare reactions with no trigger, and they all aim at 没关系/没事. Review 5 reads as a sentence made up to fit 好 and 没关系 into one prompt.

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 swaps the chunk slots cleanly (T16–T26). Round 3 is good: two or three linked sentences that use met words well (T36 "Minha irmã mais nova está doente. Acho que ela quer beber água. Amanhã nós vamos ao hospital."). Round 2 slips in places. Prompt 3 ("O pai do meu amigo é médico.", T30) and prompt 4 ("Acho que o médico está no hospital.", T32) have no time or place. Prompt 5 (T34) is an exchange ("Não foi nada! Meu irmão mais novo não está doente."), which the skill keeps for learners with fewer than ten words. Also, 病 on its own is the expected answer in only two prompts (R1.2 T18, R3.2 T38). The skill asks for at least three, though other prompts would accept 病了.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]`. I checked every prompt and both scripts: all words come from Lessons 01–07 or 22 (大 is from 06, 和 from 07, 朋友 and 的 from 05).

**5. Corrections: 3.**
- Wrong correction: T10, "> 没有关系。 Aqui entra **没** (*méi*), não 没有: **没关系** é a expressão fixa." 没有关系 is a valid, common variant and should have been accepted.
- Format slip: T28 turns the learner's pinyin answer into Hanzi in the blockquote ("Em Hanzi, você escreveu: > 爸爸明天医院看病。"). The blockquote should show what the learner actually wrote.
- The other corrections are accurate, point at one fix each, and show the full sentence once: T16 不事 to 没事, T22 time word, T34 在 not 是, T40 不想, T46 生病了.
- Pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T20, T36, T44), and the keyboard reminder comes once at the first pinyin answer (T12).

**6. Listening: 5.** The dictation has four single-turn sentences (T48). The narration "Um dia ruim" has six first-person sentences, about 25 seconds, with no listener addressed and no quotes. Questions are sent with the clip and marked one by one (T52). Every new word appears in the listening step (病 inside 大病).

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** T2 proposes the route and asks the jump-start question. T4 confirms both. The five steps come in order, and round 3 ends with the extra-round-or-listening offer (T46). The closing (T52) is "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 2, parte 1 de 2. Quer começar agora?" No wrap-up is offered, which is correct for a one-part lesson, and there is no comment on how the lesson was divided.

**8. Session length: about 55 minutes.** That is 6 review prompts, the vocabulary step, 15 round prompts, and two clips with answers. It fits the one-hour target.

**Verdict.** The learner gets a well-paced, accurate lesson on talking about being sick. It respects the jump-ahead word limits, has strong round 3 sequences, and has a clean listening step. The weak points are a false correction (没有关系 rejected), a review padded with three near-identical reassurance prompts, and a round 2 that partly falls back to bare statements and exchanges. The single change that would help most: tell the tutor to accept standard variants before marking an answer wrong (see change 2 below).

## run-2

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Mostly natural. Stilted lines:
- T4 Review 1: 谢谢你的茶！ is understandable but sounds translated. Fix: **谢谢！——不客气！** Or, to keep 茶, **谢谢你请我喝茶！** (but 请 + person + verb may be beyond the met patterns, so the plain 谢谢 is safer).
- T12 Review 5: 我爸爸的茶很好！ Natives say a drink 好喝, not 好. Fix: drop this prompt, or test 好 another way, such as **你好！** or **好，没关系。**
- T48 narration: 我很高兴，明天想吃饭。 This is a little flat. **明天我想好好吃饭。** is more natural, though 好好 adds a pattern not taught. Acceptable as written.
- 你没事吗？ (T14, T40) is acceptable. 你没事吧？ is more idiomatic, but 吧 is from Lesson 16, so the tutor was right to avoid it.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is a full line or a short dash exchange. There are no fragments or scene descriptions (T4 "Obrigado pelo chá! — De nada!", T40 "Você está bem? Acho que você está doente. Por favor, vá ao hospital ver o médico!").

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 uses the three chunks with swapped slots (T16–T26). Round 3 has five good two- or three-sentence sequences (T36–T46). Round 2 is uneven:
- Prompt 1 (T26, "Ontem eu fiquei doente, hoje não quero comer.") is already a sequence.
- Prompt 4 (T32, "Você acha que o médico é chinês?") has no time or place.
- Prompt 5 (T34) is a dash exchange.

Each new word appears in at least three prompts.

**4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]`. 请 (Lesson 02), 中国人 (04), 多 and 大 (06), and 家 (07) are all met. Nothing from Lessons 08–21.

**5. Corrections: 5.** Each correction quotes the whole answer, names one fix in bold with pinyin, and gives the full sentence once:
- T10: 不客气 to 没关系, with a clear reason.
- T16: 吗 moved to the end.
- T22: missing 是.
- T32: 我和爸爸 before the verb.
- T34: 中国人 not 中国.
- T40: 她 not 他.
- T46: time word.
- T50: missing 也.

The missed items come back (没关系 at T34, time word order in round 3). Pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T20, T28, T36, T44), and the keyboard tip comes once (T12).

**6. Listening: 5.** The dictation has five single-turn sentences (T48). The narration "No hospital" is five first-person sentences, about 20 seconds, which is at the low end of the range but within it. There is no listener and no quote, and the questions come with the clip. Every new word appears. The answers are marked one by one, with the wrong one explained (T52 "Errado: foi a mãe (**妈妈**).").

**7. Flow and closing: 5.** T2 asks both questions (jump start, what comes next) in one message, and T4 confirms. The steps come in order. The closing (T52) is "Próxima vez: HSK 1, Aula 2, parte 1. Quer começar agora?" It leaves out "de 2", which is a trivial gap, and it says nothing about how the lesson was divided.

**8. Session length: about 55 minutes.** The structure matches run-1, with a slightly longer dictation. It fits the hour.

**Verdict.** This is the cleanest of the three. Every correction is accurate and correctly formatted, the round 3 sequences are rich, and the listening step is complete. The learner leaves able to say who is sick, who goes to the hospital, and what they think of the doctor, using only words they know. The flaws are two stilted review lines (谢谢你的茶, 茶很好) and a round 2 that mixes in a sequence, an exchange and a prompt with no time or place. The single change that would help most: tighten the round 2 rule so that all five prompts carry who, when or where, and what (change 1 below).

## run-3

**1. Natural Mandarin: 3.** The round answers are natural, but the narration, which is the model the learner hears, has two unnatural lines and a gap in logic:
- T48 narration: 今天我妈妈觉得我要去医院。 The intended meaning is "my mother thinks I should go", but 觉得我要去 reads as "thinks I'm going to go". Fix: **妈妈觉得我病了。** (met words), followed by 我和妈妈去医院看病。
- T48 narration: 医生是女士，她很好。 Using 女士 as a bare predicate is stilted. Fix: **医生是妈妈的朋友，她很好。** or simply **医生很好。**
- T48 narration: 我妈妈很高兴。 No reason is given, so the line has no logical place in the story. Fix: give a cause with met words, for example **医生觉得我没事，妈妈很高兴。**
- T20 Round 1.3: 我觉得汉语很好。 This is grammatical but vague ("the Chinese language is good"). **我觉得你的汉语很好。** sounds more like real speech.

**2. Whole sentences: 4.** Every prompt is a line, but some of the Portuguese is clumsy. Round 1.2 (T18) reads "Meu irmão mais velho vai ao hospital ir ao médico.", and the chunk gloss at T16 reads "Vou ao hospital ir ao médico." Neither is natural Portuguese. **4. Only met words: 5.** No `[NÃO SEI]`. 汉语 (04), 个 (07), 女士 (05), 也 (03) and 要 (02) are all met. Nothing from Lessons 08–21.

**5. Corrections: 5.** All are accurate and in the right format:

Each missed item comes back later (also in R3.2, 了 in R3.5, 觉得 in R3.3). The keyboard tip comes once at the first pinyin answer (T12).

**Verdict.** The learner gets accurate corrections and a sound lesson, kept within the jump-ahead word limits. ## Cross-run summary

**Problems in more than one run. These point at the instructions:**
- **Round 2 drifts in all three runs.** Every run has round 2 prompts with no time or place, and every run puts a dash exchange in round 2 (run-1 T34, run-2 T34, run-3 T30), even though the learner has met far more than ten words. Run-2 also opens round 2 with what is really a sequence.
- **The same frame is overused.** All runs repeat "X (amanhã/hoje) vai ao hospital ver o médico" across round 1 and round 2. In run-3 it appears three times almost word for word.
- **Narration coherence.** The narrations in run-1 (没有大病，妈妈很高兴) and run-3 (妈妈很高兴 with no cause, 觉得我要去, 医生是女士) contain sentences that are unlinked or unnatural.
- **Thin review for a part of reactions.** The previous part (不客气 对不起 没关系 您 好 没事) is mostly replies. Runs 1 and 2 padded the review with contrived lines (run-1 Review 5, run-2 谢谢你的茶 / 茶很好). Run-3 handled it best with short dash exchanges.
- **Closing label.** Runs 2 and 3 write "Aula 2, parte 1" without "de 2" (minor).
- **Vocabulary table.** All runs use Markdown, not the HTML template. This may be the environment rather than the skill.

**Problems seen only once:**
- run-1: a valid answer was rejected (没有关系, T10). A pinyin answer was shown as Hanzi in the blockquote (T28). 病 on its own was expected in only two prompts.
- run-2: stilted review lines (谢谢你的茶, 我爸爸的茶很好).
- run-3: clumsy Portuguese in the prompts ("vai ao hospital ir ao médico"), and the unnatural narration sentences listed above.

**The three changes I would make first:**

1. **`SKILL.md`, round 2.** Change "**Round 2 · Full actions.** Each prompt is a complete sentence with who, when or where, and what happens, joining a new chunk to words met earlier." to: "**Round 2 · Full actions.** Each of the five prompts is one sentence that names who, when or where, and what happens, joining a new chunk to words met earlier. Once the learner has met ten words, round 2 has no exchanges and no two-sentence prompts, and no two prompts in round 1 or 2 share the same frame with only the subject or time swapped."

2. **`lesson-format.md`, written translation.** Change "**Correct and natural.** Confirm briefly and move to the next prompt. Accept any natural version that meets the request." to add: "Before marking an answer incorrect, check whether it is a standard variant, such as 没有关系 for 没关系 or 没有生病 for 没生病. A standard variant is correct: confirm it, and if you wanted the other form, name it in one line."

3. **`lesson-format.md`, narration.** Change "One person speaking in the first person about their own day, plan, or something that happened, in a concrete scene: three to eight sentences, 20 to 40 seconds at a clear pace." to add: "Each sentence follows from the one before, and every feeling or decision has its cause stated in met words (医生觉得我没事，妈妈很高兴). If a meaning such as 'should' has no met word, leave it out rather than forcing a near-synonym."

A fourth candidate, if the three above are already done: in `SKILL.md`, under "1. **Review.**", add "When the previous part's words are replies (不客气, 没关系, 没事), write each as a short dash exchange with its natural trigger rather than as a bare reaction or a sentence made up to hold two items." Run-3's review shows this works.
