### Problems in both runs (point at the instructions)

- **站 is taught almost only inside 地铁站.** run-a uses it alone once in a prompt, run-b only in the narration. The "three prompts" count is met by the compound.
- **X 离 Y 远 dominates.** Much stronger in run-a, but in both the chunk list is three variations of one pattern plus 坐地铁去.
- **Listening slips and To reinforce.** The runs chose differently: run-a saved 离 and 后面 after dictation and narration misses, run-b saved nothing after the 饭馆 miss. The rule doesn't say whether listening counts.
- **Small naturalness habits the checklist doesn't name:** 我的公司 (run-a), noun compared with action (run-b).

### Problems seen once

- run-a: dictation lines repeating prompt frames; narration drifting from plan to past; 我在前面等你 with no reference point; a review prompt below level (review 4).
- run-b: a concession with weak logic (review 3).

### Three changes to make first

1. `SKILL.md`, "Every prompt": *"**A new frame.** In the whole part, review included, no two prompts share a frame with only the subject, time, place, or name swapped."* Add: "A clause counts as a frame, and so do the dictation and narration lines. When a part teaches a pattern (A 离 B 远), use it in at most half the round prompts and say the same idea other ways with met words (从…到…, 多远, 住得远)."
2. `SKILL.md`, "The five steps", step 3: *"Across them, every new word appears in at least three prompts."* Add: "A new word that is also part of a compound (站 in 地铁站) is used on its own in at least one of them (两站, 到站)."
3. `SKILL.md`, "The course file": *"After each part, remove the items the learner got right unaided during the part, then add the ones they missed and didn't get right later."* Add: "A word misheard in the dictation or missed in a narration answer counts as missed." And in "Every prompt", extend *"**Natural.** The answer is what a mainland speaker would say aloud: everyday verbs, time words before the verb, a subject that can really do the verb."* with: "the place you work or study is 我们公司, 我们学校; a comparison sets like against like (坐地铁比开车快)."
