### 1. Natural Mandarin: 3

Nothing is ungrammatical and all pinyin is right (bú kèqi, duìbuqǐ, méi guānxi, méishì, xièxie, neutral tones correct). The problem is that about a third of the lines are words placed side by side to reach the "three prompts per word" count, not things a mainland speaker says in one breath. Every questionable line:

| Turn | Line | Problem | Fix |

| T16 | 你好！谢谢！再见！ | A whole encounter compressed into one speaker's line; nobody says this. It is also T12 + T14 glued together. | Drop the prompt (the skill says give fewer), or use an exchange: — 谢谢！ — 再见！ is still weak; with 3 words, 4 prompts is the honest count. |

### Verdict

A learner gets a clean, well-paced first session: setup is clear, the table and notes are accurate, corrections are short and point at the one thing that went wrong, the 你/您 and 不客气/没关系 contrasts are drilled and tracked, and the closings and saved file are right. What they do not get is consistently real Mandarin: to reach five prompts per round and three uses per word with nine words, the tutor glues reply words together (没关系，不客气; 好，不客气，再见; 您好，对不起), so the learner practises lines no one says while a listed chunk (好，谢谢) is never practised at all. The single change that would help most: in the "fewer than ten words" section, say that the three-prompts-per-word count yields to naturalness, and forbid a line that stacks two reactions or opens with 好 when nothing has been proposed.

## Cross-run summary

Only one run, so nothing can be separated into "repeated" and "seen once"; the points below are the ones that look caused by the instructions rather than by chance, because each follows from two rules pulling against each other.

Three changes I would make first:

1. **`SKILL.md`, "While the learner has met fewer than ten words"**, line to change:
   > "Every new word still appears in at least three prompts."

   It conflicts with "give fewer" two lines above and is what produces 没关系，不客气 and 好，对不起. Change to: "Every new word still appears in at least three prompts, unless that needs a line no one would say; then two is enough. A line never stacks two reactions (not 没关系，不客气), and never opens with 好 unless the line before it proposed something."

2. **`SKILL.md`, step 2 and round 1**, line to change:
   > "**Round 1 · Chunks.** Each prompt uses one of the part's chunks with a slot changed (the person, the place, the time, what follows the verb), never the chunk or its example as listed."

   Add: "Every listed chunk is used in at least one round-1 prompt. A chunk is never a bare word from the table: while the learner has met five words or fewer, the chunks are the natural two-word lines (谢谢，再见！), and the single words are not asked on their own." This removes T8's "Olá!" → 你好！ and makes 好，谢谢！ unskippable.

3. **`lesson-format.md`, Dictation**, line to change:
   > "No sentence repeats a chunk or a prompt of the part, holds both sides of an exchange (not 谢谢，不客气), or doubles a word to fill a line."

   Change to: "No sentence repeats a chunk or a prompt of the part, holds both sides of an exchange (not 谢谢，不客气), answers the sentence before it, or adds a greeting or 好 only to fill the line (not 您好，对不起 or 好，不客气，再见)."

A smaller fourth point, for `requests.md` (wrap-up role-play): "Correct each reply as a written translation" led the tutor to reject a natural reply (没关系 after 谢谢). Add: "accept any reply a native speaker could give at that point; if it avoids the lesson's expression, name that expression in one line."
