# Run notes: s1-new-course, round 5, run 1

Scenario: s1-new-course. Turns: 64 (T1 to T64). Stopped after the wrap-up role-play ended. Final state saved in course/course.md (Next: Lesson 02 part 1, unchanged by the role-play).

Unclear points or doubtful applications:

1. Language of setup. The first message `start mandarinmaxxing` is English. SKILL.md: "If there is too little text, use the language the interface or what you already know about the learner points to, or ask in one short line before the setup." I used Portuguese, based on what was already known about the learner (stored profile), and did not ask. Without that, the rule would have forced an extra question turn.

2. Chunks with no open slot. lesson-format.md / SKILL.md round 1: "Each prompt uses one of the part's chunks, swapping its open slot". Part 1 (你好 / 谢谢 / 再见) has no chunk with a slot, so round 1 prompts combine two chunks (你好，谢谢). In part 2 only 好，谢谢 has a swappable slot. "Never ask for a chunk exactly as listed" was kept.

3. Offer after the rounds when round 3 is skipped. "After round 3, offer one more round in the style of round 3, or the listening step." With round 3 skipped (fewer than ten words met), I still offered "one more round like this one or the listening step" after round 1 (part 1) and after round 2 (part 2). Not explicit that the offer applies.

4. Round 1 only vs "five met words or fewer". Part 1 has 3 met words: round 1 only. Part 2 reaches 9 met words (3 + 6): I counted the part's new words as met and gave round 1 plus a round 2 of short exchanges, no round 3. Quote: "While the learner has met fewer than ten words ... skip round 3." Unclear whether the count includes the part's own new words (it makes a difference only at the 5-word and 10-word edges).

5. Review of a part with fewer than five words. Part 2's review followed "When that part had fewer than five words, fill the review with the items to reinforce and the older words ... when there is nothing older to bring back, one prompt per word is enough." I gave 3 prompts (one per part-1 word, the reinforced 再见 first), each one word, which conflicts with "at least five and at most ten" and "written at the level of the course", but the exception seems to cover it. Also "round-2-length sentence" cannot be built from 3 met words.

6. "Every new word appears in at least three prompts" for 没关系 and 没事. They translate the same ("não foi nada"), so contexts that fit only one are not possible with the met words. Even with prompts asking for one by name, in the ten prompts of rounds 1 and 2 I could only place each of 没关系 and 没事 in two prompts (plus the dictation and role-play). The other four words reach three or more. The rule (usage-note paragraph: "give each prompt a context that fits only one ... or accept both") does not say how to meet the three-prompt minimum for such a pair. Asking for a word by name inside the prompt ("Use 没事") is my own device based on "ask for the other by name".

7. Dictation with 3 words (part 1). "no sentence repeats a chunk or any prompt of the part as written" is hard with only 你好, 谢谢, 再见: I used doubled words (你好，你好！) so no sentence equals a chunk or a round prompt. Three sentences is the minimum allowed.

8. Narration. "a narration clip once the met words can carry about 20 seconds": with 3 and 9 met words I judged that it could not, so the listening step was the dictation alone in both parts. Dictation numbering restarted at 1 in each part (the skill says "its number" without saying whether it restarts).

9. Closing message of part 1 / wrap-up. Part 1 is not the last part of Lesson 1, so no wrap-up offer there; at the end of part 2 the closing offered both the role-play and the next part, and the student chose the role-play. After the role-play I did not repeat the offer to start the next part now ("Offer it once" covers the role-play only).

10. To reinforce handling. After part 1 I saved 再见 (missed in the dictation; the earlier miss in round 1 was answered right in later prompts). After part 2 I removed 再见 (answered right in review) and added 没关系 (dictation slip); 你好 and 您 were not added because the student got them right later in the same part. The slip of order in 好，没关系 was also answered right later (round 2, prompt 4). Whether a dictation miss, which can't be retried, should always be added was not explicit.

11. Meaning of 好 in the table. Before part 2's table the student cannot know 好 alone; so the review mistake was limited to the part 1 words. 好 is given in the table as "bom; bem; certo (ok)" and used in chunks like 好，谢谢. This follows the table but 好 alone as "certo" is a usage the skill never sets out.

12. Pinyin of 没事: the syllabus gives "méishì" (one word) while the map and usual teaching materials sometimes write "méi shì". I followed the syllabus.
