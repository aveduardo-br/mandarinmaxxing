### 2. Whole sentences: 3

With fewer than ten met words the skill allows one-speaker lines and short exchanges, so interjection lines are in scope. But several prompts are not things anyone says: T10 "Olá, tchau!", T12 "Olá, obrigado, tchau!", T14 "Tchau, obrigado!" (a shuffle of T8 "Obrigado, tchau!", which the skill forbids: "Never repeat a word inside a prompt or shuffle lines just to make a new prompt"). Part 2's review (T22–T26) is three bare words, "Tchau!", "Obrigado!", "Olá!". Part 2's rounds are good: "Olá, senhor! Desculpe!" (T30), "Obrigado, senhor! — De nada, tchau!" (T46) are real lines.

### 3. Round progression: 4

The tutor applied the low-word rules correctly: part 1 (3 met words) got round 1 only, no round 2 or 3; part 2 (9 met words) got round 1, then a round 2 of exchanges whose second line reacts to the first (T38 "Obrigado! — De nada!", T40 "Desculpe! — Não foi nada!"), and round 3 was skipped. Round 1 of part 2 swaps the slot of 好，谢谢 (T28 好，再见; T34 好，没关系).

Problems:
- Coverage: "every new word appears in at least three prompts." In part 2, 没关系 is expected in only two prompts (T34, T44) and 没事 in only two (T36, T40); T34 accepted either. The synonym pair shares the slots instead of each getting three.
- T42 "Olá, senhora! — Olá!" is close to the echo the skill rules out ("Hi! — Hi!"); it works only for the 您/你 contrast.
- Part 1 round 1 has no real chunk slot to swap (the chunks are single words), so its five prompts are combinations and shuffles of three words (see dim 2).

The word-order slip at T36 shows the right order. Deductions:
- T28: the miss came on the last review prompt, and the skill says "add a prompt only if none is left"; none was added, so 你好 got no immediate retry (it comes back only inside T42's second line).
- T36: "没关系，好！" was marked as a word-order error. The prompt order was "Certo, não foi nada", so the call is defensible, but 没关系，好 is itself a possible Mandarin utterance; a lighter "natural, but I asked for 好 first" would have fit the rule "judge the learner's answer by whether it is natural Mandarin for that meaning".

### 9. Level fit: 3

Part 2's review (T22–T26: "Tchau!", "Obrigado!", "Olá!") is simpler than part 1's own round 1 ("Olá, obrigado, tchau!"), so the course steps backward at the start of part 2. With three met words little is possible, but two-word lines in a real order (谢谢，再见！) were available. Part 2's rounds rise properly with the 您 lines (T30, T46).

### Verdict

A learner gets a clean, correctly paced start: twelve words in about half an hour, chunks that work, careful corrections, and a fitting role-play, with the low-word rules for rounds applied as written. What drags it down is padding: when the tutor had too few words to make five distinct natural prompts or three dictation lines, it shuffled and doubled them (Olá, tchau!; 再见，谢谢 after 谢谢，再见; 你好，你好！). **Single change:** let a part with five met words or fewer give fewer than five round prompts and fewer dictation lines, and forbid shuffled or doubled lines.

## Cross-run summary

Only one transcript was graded, so nothing here can be confirmed as recurring across runs. Problems seen in this run:
- Padding when the met words are very few: shuffled prompts and a goodbye-before-thanks order (T10–T14), doubled dictation lines (T18, T50).
- Synonym pairs splitting the three-prompt minimum (没关系 and 没事 got two prompts each).
- An early review weaker than the previous part's rounds (T22–T26).
- One-off: no added prompt after a miss on the last review item (T28).

Three changes to make first:

1. `SKILL.md`, the line "With five met words or fewer, give round 1 only: an echo such as “Hi! — Hi!” is not an exchange." Add: "Keep the order of a real encounter in every line, and when the words can't make five different natural lines, give fewer prompts rather than reversing, shuffling or repeating one."
2. `lesson-format.md`, the line "**Dictation.** Three to five short sentences, each a thing one person says in a single turn, built from the part’s words and older ones, at the level of round 2: no sentence repeats a chunk or any prompt of the part as written." Add: "Never double a word to fill a line (对不起，对不起); with very few met words, two or three real lines are enough."
3. `SKILL.md`, the line "Across the rounds, every new word appears in at least three prompts." Add: "Each word of a synonym pair counts on its own: a prompt that accepts either counts for neither." The review rule should also say that in a course's first parts a review prompt is at least as long as the longest prompt of the part reviewed.
