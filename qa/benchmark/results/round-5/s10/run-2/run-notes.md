# Run notes: s10-synonyms, run-2

Scenario: s10-synonyms (HSK 1, Lesson 4 part 3: 明天 会1 中文 昨天 汉语). Turns: 48 (T1 to T48), stopped after the closing message.

Places where an instruction was unclear or I was unsure how to apply it:

1. "When the learner keeps choosing one (中文 over 汉语), ask for the other by name in the next prompt that fits and again in the next part's review." Unclear how many choices count as "keeps choosing". I asked after the first 中文 answer (round 1, prompt 3). Also, "every new word appears in at least three prompts" has no natural context where only 汉语 fits, so I asked for 汉语 by name in three prompts (R1 4, R2 5, R3 2) with a plain line "Use 汉语 para “chinês”". Not sure that line is allowed given "never ... a stage direction in parentheses" (I did not use parentheses).
2. "Never repeat a word inside a prompt" / "no two prompts share the same frame with only the subject, the time, the place or a name swapped": with about 45 known words and these new ones, it was hard to avoid near-repeats (e.g. 昨天他在哪儿 vs 昨天她在中国).
3. "Rounds 1 and 2 have one speaker and one sentence per prompt": several round 2 prompts have two clauses joined by a comma (他是中国人，昨天他在中国). Unsure if a comma clause counts as one sentence.
4. Labels line lacked Ditado, Áudio and Rounds ("If an older file lacks one, choose it once and save it right away"). I chose Rounds: Blocos, Ações completas, Sequências and added Ditado, Áudio, and saved them in the final course.md only (no real mid-session write was made).
5. Review size: "one prompt per new word of the previous part ... at least five and at most ten". The previous part had six words; I used five prompts (哪 and 国 share one prompt) and wove in the older words 是 and 对不起 (line 3 and 7 back) and the item to reinforce 什么.
6. "To reinforce" rule: it says to add items missed and not answered right unaided later in the part. 汉语 (dictation slip, 中文 written for 汉语) was the only one left. I also kept it as the synonym to ask for in the next part's review, but the skill says that is a separate rule, not tied to the list.
7. "Keep the script out of the message" versus the runner's rule to write the audio-script block for the clip: I wrote the script only inside the audio-script block, with no pinyin or translation until after the learner answered.
8. Narration question "what a chunk meant in context": I quoted only 会 in the question (not the sentence) to avoid exposing the script. The skill does not say how much of a chunk the question may show.
9. Spelling of 会 in the Done line: I used the map's 会1 and showed plain 会 to the learner, as the skill says.
