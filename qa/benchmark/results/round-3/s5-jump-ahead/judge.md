# Judge · round 3 · s5-jump-ahead

Scenario: the learner has finished HSK 1 Lesson 1 and jumps to Lesson 22 (Getting sick, one part at pace 5: 病 看病 医生 生病 觉得 医院). The tutor should ask whether to count the jump start as known and confirm what comes after Lesson 22. Prompts may use only jump-start words (Lessons 01 to 07) plus Lesson 22, never words from Lessons 08 to 21.

Standard: `skills/mandarinmaxxing/SKILL.md` and `lesson-format.md`. The simulated student is not graded.

## Run 1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 5.** Expected answers, corrections and scripts are natural mainland Mandarin, with time words before the verb throughout (T10, T40 fix exactly that). Minor polish only:
- Review 3 (T8): 我爸爸和妈妈明天去中国 → **我爸爸妈妈明天去中国** reads more smoothly; the original is still correct.
- Narration (T52): 昨天我妈妈病了，今天我们去看病 makes 我们 the patients; **今天我们和妈妈去医院** or simply **今天妈妈去看病** keeps the patient clear with met words.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is a line someone would say: "Com licença, o senhor é chinês?" (T10), "Mamãe, eu estou no hospital. O médico acha que eu estou bem." (T40).

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 swaps the slots properly in most prompts: 我→我弟弟 (T18), 他→我爸爸 (T20), adds 明天 (T22). Weaknesses: Round 1 · 4 "Acho que ela ficou doente" (T24) is the exact example printed in the chunk's meaning line; Round 1 · 5 (T26) uses 没有病, which is not one of the chunks. Round 2 has complete actions with time or place ("Ontem minha mãe foi ao hospital ver o médico", T28). Round 3 has connected two- and three-sentence prompts, but the frame 去医院看病 with only the subject or time swapped appears in R1·3, R2·1, R3·1, R3·3 and the dictation, and "今天不在家，在医院" is repeated in R3·4 and R3·5.

**4. Only met words: 5.** 0 prompts need an unmet word.

**5. Corrections: 5.** Every correction follows the format: "Quase! Você escreveu:", the blockquote, one fix line in bold with pinyin, then the full sentence in three lines (T10, T16, T22, T28, T34, T40, T46). Each fix is correct and points at one thing only (都 in T16, 个 in T34, 在 in T46). Pinyin answers are confirmed with Hanzi (T12, T20, T36, T44), and the keyboard reminder appears once (T12). Errors come back later: the time-word slip of T10 is tested again in R3·1, and 医院/医生 in R2·1 and R3·4.

**6. Listening: 4.** Dictation has five single-turn sentences, the script is hidden until the learner answers, the correction uses blockquote + fix line + full script (T52). The narration is first person and uses every new word across the step. Two problems: (a) the questions (T52) are all "what happened / who" questions; none asks what the speaker wanted or plans, or what a chunk means in context, while `lesson-format.md` asks for "at least one of each kind"; (b) the narration contains reported speech (她说我妈妈没事), which sits close to the "never quotes anyone" rule.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** Five steps in order, closing correct and short (T54). Deduction: the jump-start review should take "five to ten jump-start words that fit the new lesson's situation", but its prompts are about going to China (T8, T12), asking where someone wants to go (T4) and being a Chinese national (T10). Only 没事 and the family words relate to getting sick.

**8. Session length.** 7 review prompts, 15 round prompts, a 5-sentence dictation and a narration with 5 questions: about 50 minutes. That fits the hour. The tutor offers the next part.

**9. Level fit: 4.** Most review prompts are two-clause sentences at round-2 length ("Meus irmãos mais novos estavam em casa ontem, hoje não estão", T6; "Pai, nós todos queremos beber água, ninguém quer comer", T16). Review 1 (你今天想去哪儿？) and Review 4 (请问，您是中国人吗？) are first-lesson length.

**Verdict.** A learner gets a clean, correct part: natural sentences, precise corrections, steady practice of 去医院看病 and 觉得 + clause, and a fair listening step. What's weak is variety and situational focus: the review wanders away from illness, and the hospital frame repeats with only the subject or time changed. **Most useful change:** make the jump-start review pick words for the new lesson's situation and write them at round-2 length (see change 2 below).

- Review 4 (T10): 没事！我妈妈和我今天也在家 joins "Não foi nada!" to a sentence it doesn't answer. Nobody says this. Fix: drop the reaction, or make it one situation: **没事，我今天在家，不去医院。**

## Cross-run summary

**Problems in more than one run (point at the instructions):**
- **Round 1 copies the chunks unchanged.** Run 2 (3 of 5), run 3 (3 of 5), and run 1 reuses the example printed under a chunk (R1·4). The instruction "swapping its open slot" is being read as optional.
- **Short, first-lesson review prompts after the jump.** All three runs mix two-clause sentences with lines like 你今天想去哪儿, 您请喝水, 请问，您叫什么名字. The jump-start review clause says nothing about length, and the nearby "one prompt per word is enough" fallback seems to pull prompts down to formula size.
- **The jump-start review doesn't fit the situation.** Strongest in run 1 (China, nationality); present in run 3 (asking a name). Run 2 did this best.
- **The same frame repeats across rounds.** 去医院看病 with only the subject or time swapped (runs 1, 2, 3), and 不想吃饭，也不想喝水 (runs 2, 3). The anti-repetition rule covers only rounds 1 and 2.
- **Round 2 prompts without a time or place, or with two sentences** (runs 2 and 3).
- **我妈妈和我 / 我爸爸和我 word order** in expected answers (runs 2 and 3). This is minor, but the Mandarin check misses it.
- **Markdown table instead of the HTML template** in all three. This may be a harness limit, so check it before treating it as a skill problem.

**Problems seen once:** an incoherent review prompt (run 2, Review 4); "Largada (aulas 1 a 7)" said although Lesson 1 is done (run 2, T2); narration questions missing the plan and chunk-meaning kinds, plus reported speech in the narration (run 1); a narration probably shorter than 20 seconds (run 3).

**Three changes to make first:**

1. `SKILL.md`, round 1. Change
   > "**Round 1 · Chunks.** Each prompt uses one of the part’s chunks, swapping its open slot, so the learner feels how the chunk works."

   to add: "Never ask for a chunk exactly as listed, or for the example in its meaning line: change at least one slot (the person, the place, the time, or what follows 觉得)."

2. `SKILL.md`, review step. Change
   > "After a jump past an unfinished jump start, with `Known: jump start`, the first review takes five to ten jump-start words that fit the new lesson’s situation, instead of the last **Done** line."

   to add: "Write each one as a round-2 sentence set in or near the new situation (for Lesson 22: staying home, not eating, thanking someone, saying where a family member is), never as a greeting or introduction formula."

3. `SKILL.md`, rounds. Change
   > "Once the learner has met ten words, rounds 1 and 2 have one speaker and one sentence per prompt, and no two prompts share the same frame with only the subject or the time swapped."

   to: "…and no two prompts anywhere in the part (review, rounds, dictation) share the same frame with only the subject or the time swapped. Every round-2 prompt names when or where."
