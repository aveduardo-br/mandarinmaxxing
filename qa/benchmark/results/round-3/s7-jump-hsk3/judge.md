## Run 1

**1. Natural Mandarin: 4.** Almost every expected answer is natural mainland Mandarin (T4 我的邻居去年去中国了，到现在还没回来; T8 我爱人在医院工作，所以晚上才回家; T48 narration). Problems:
- T12 (Review 5) expected 我和朋友喜欢一样的菜 → **我和朋友喜欢吃一样的菜** (喜欢 + food needs the verb here).
- T41 (Round 3 · 4) expected 可是我学了很多 for "estudei bastante" (prepared a lot) is a little off → **可是我学习了很长时间** or **可是我准备了很久**.
- T34 overstates a rule: the learner's 我妹妹觉得数学太难，想请老师帮忙 is acceptable as it stands. 太…了 is the usual exclamation, but inside a 觉得 clause a bare 太难 is fine. The tutor's version is fine too; the correction just wasn't needed.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** Every prompt is a real line, for example T4 "Meu vizinho foi para a China no ano passado e até agora ainda não voltou." and T35 "Minha nota de história não foi boa. Por isso, agora eu estudo todos os dias…". No fragments or stage directions.

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 practices the three chunks, with slot swaps in 1.2 (汉语不太容易), 1.3 (历史成绩提高了) and 1.4 (提高数学成绩). But 1.1 (T14 "A matemática é muito difícil." → 数学很难) is the chunk word for word, and 1.5 (T23 历史很容易，可是数学很难) is already two clauses. Round 2 gives complete actions with who/where/when (T24 我爸爸在学校工作…; T31 昨天的历史考试很容易…). Round 3 gives connected two- or three-sentence accounts (T36–T44).

**4. Only met words: 5.** 0 prompts need an unmet word. Borderline items are all fine: 题 (HSK 2) in the narration, 帮忙 and 考试 (HSK 2), 只是/总是/比较 (Done).

**5. Corrections: 4.** The format is right every time: blockquote, one fix line in bold with pinyin, full sentence once (T10 **在医院工作**; T22 **提高数学成绩**; T28 **每天** before the verb, on a pinyin answer). Pinyin answers are confirmed with the Hanzi (T12, T20, T36, T44), and the keyboard reminder comes once (T12). Corrected items come back later: 提高 + object in T26, 每天学习 in T35. Docked for the unneeded 太…了 correction at T34.

**6. Listening: 5.** Dictation (T46) has four single-speaker sentences, the script appears only after the answer, and the one wrong sentence gets blockquote + fix line + full script (T48). Narration (T48, "Dia de prova") is first person, five sentences (~25 s), with no listener addressed and no quote. 老师告诉我，我的成绩提高了 is indirect report, which is acceptable. Five questions cover what happened, plans and meaning in context, answers are taken together and each is marked (T50). Every new word appears across the two clips.

**7. Flow and closing: 4.** The tutor confirms what comes after the jump (T2) and asks no jump-start question, which is right. The five steps come in order, "Traduza para o mandarim" appears in the right two places, and the title is handled correctly with review first. The closing (T50) is "Próxima vez: HSK 3, Aula 30, parte 2. Quer começar agora?", with no wrap-up offer because the lesson isn't finished (correct) and no comment on the division. Docked because the older word **发生** (14 back) never appears in the review or the rounds. 阿姨 (T6) and 只是 (T12) do.

**8. Session length: ~45 min.** 5 review prompts, a vocabulary message, 15 round prompts (round 3 answers are long), a 4-sentence dictation and a 5-question narration. That is a little short of the hour, as expected for a 6-word part, and the closing offers the next part.

**9. Level fit: 5.** The review is fully at round-2 level even though it reviews Lesson 6: T6 商店的阿姨很热情，我去的时候她总是帮我; T10 昨天那个男人突然叫了我的名字，可是我不认识他. Rounds 2–3 use 为了, 以前…现在, 后来 and 应该.

**Verdict.** A solid, clean part. The learner gets a review that sounds like HSK 3, correct and focused corrections, and a good listening step, with no unmet words anywhere. The real gaps are a missing older word (发生) and a round 1 that opens with a verbatim chunk. The skill change that would help most: make the older-word picks a checklist the review has to tick off before the vocabulary step.

## Run 2

**1. Natural Mandarin: 3.** The review and rounds 1–2 are natural (T4 昨天我邻居家发生了一件事情，可是我不知道; T7 超市的阿姨很热情，常常和我聊天儿). Problems:
- T43 (Round 3 · 5) expected 老师今天说了数学成绩。我提高了，可是历史成绩还不太好 is unnatural. 说了数学成绩 is odd, and a bare 我提高了 doesn't say what improved. Fix: **今天老师告诉我们数学成绩了。我的数学成绩提高了，可是历史成绩还不太好。** The tutor then accepted the learner's matching sentences as correct (T44 "As outras duas frases estão certas").

- T41 expected 我每天晚上要学习历史 is fine, but 成绩就会提高 (offered at T42) is the more natural version, and the tutor said so correctly.
- T48 narration 我想问老师，怎么提高历史成绩: the comma breaks an embedded question → **我想问老师怎么提高历史成绩**.

**2. Whole sentences: 5.** All prompts are real lines ("Minha colega disse que a aula de história é muito difícil, mas a de matemática é fácil.").

**3. Round progression: 4.** Round 1 swaps slots properly (1.3 他的数学成绩; 1.4 历史成绩), but 1.5 (T23 数学太难了) practices 太…了, which isn't one of the listed chunks. **5. Items come back well: 只是 is reworked into Review 5 right after the miss (T12), 容易 in T27, 要 in T41, 了 in T35. T42 handles a valid alternative well (就会). Docked for accepting 我提高了 at T44.

**6. Listening: 5.** Dictation (T46) has four single-speaker sentences. A question to someone (你的历史成绩怎么样？) is still one turn, so it's allowed. The fix for sentence 2 and the full script follow (T48). **8. Session length: ~45 min.** Same shape as run 1, with slightly shorter round 1 and dictation. It fits a short part, and the next part is offered.

**9. Level fit: 4.** The review is at round-2 level (T10 以前那个男人在医院工作，后来他变成了老师). Rounds 2–3 are varied. The dictation is first-lesson simple (我想提高数学成绩。), and a few round 3 prompts are shorter than the met words would allow (T37).

**Verdict.** A very good review, with all older words woven in, a corrected item brought straight back, and accurate, well-targeted corrections throughout. **Verdict.** Usable and broadly accurate, with good listening and well-targeted corrections, but it reads more elementary than the course: short review lines, a round 1 that mostly repeats the chunks, and an unnatural 对我很难 that a correction reinforces.
