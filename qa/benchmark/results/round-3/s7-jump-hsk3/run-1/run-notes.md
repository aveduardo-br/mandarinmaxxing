# Run notes: s7-jump-hsk3, run 1

Scenario: s7-jump-hsk3 (HSK 3 course, Lessons 1-6 done, jump start finished; student asks for Lesson 30). Turns: 50 (T1-T50). Taught Lesson 30, part 1 (数学 难 成绩 历史 容易 提高), stopped after the closing message.

Places where an instruction was unclear or I was unsure how to apply it:

1. Asking vs stating what comes after a jump.
   "Whenever the learner jumps to a lesson out of route order, confirm in one line what comes after it, and save the route." "Confirm" could mean state a default or ask. The scenario hints the tutor asks, so I asked (T2) and saved Route: 30, 07-29, 31-37 after the answer.

2. Which words the first review covers after a jump inside a level with a finished jump start.
   "One prompt per new word of the previous part in the **Done** log" and "After a jump past an unfinished jump start, with `Known: jump start`, the first review takes five to ten jump-start words..." The second case does not apply here (jump start is done), so I used the last Done line (3-06.3, five words) even though it is from Lesson 6 and unrelated to Lesson 30. The skill does not say whether the review should fit the new lesson's situation in this case.

3. Older-word distances.
   "reuse words from the lines 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 back in the Done log." The log has 18 lines, so only 3, 7 and 14 apply (阿姨, 只是, 突然). Fine, but with a jump the "older" lines are the lessons right before Lesson 7 in the route, not before Lesson 30; I followed the log literally.

4. Labels line missing entries.
   "If an older file lacks one, choose it once and save it right away." The starting course.md had no Ditado, Áudio, or Rounds line. I chose Ditado, Áudio and "Rounds: Chunks, Ações completas, Sequências" and saved them.

5. Title line plus table heading.
   "When a part starts with review, the title line opens the review and the table's heading repeats it later. With the Markdown fallback, the title goes in a bold line above the table." Followed as written: bold title at the start of the review (T4) and again above the table (T14). Also the bold round name line immediately followed by the counter line ("Bateria 1 · Chunks" then "Bateria 1 · 1 de 5") is redundant but is what the format asks for.

6. Round 1 frames.
   "no two prompts share the same frame with only the subject or the time swapped" conflicts with how few chunk patterns a 6-word part gives; I varied round 1 by mixing chunk patterns and one two-clause prompt.

7. Reworking prompts after a miss.
   "rework a prompt still to come so it uses the item that went wrong, and add a prompt only if none is left." I reworked R2-1 (在学校工作 after the 在医院工作 slip), R3-1 (每天学习), R3-4 (太...了) and R3-5 (难 vs 容易). Every classroom slip was answered right later, so none stayed in To reinforce from the rounds.

8. To reinforce after the listening step.
   The dictation slip (missing 比较) was never reused, so 比较容易 was added. For the wrong narration answer (history vs math) it was unclear whether a comprehension miss counts as an item; I added 历史. Final line: "To reinforce: 比较容易, 历史".

9. Closing message with feedback.
   Dictation feedback and the narration clip share one message (T48); the format says clips are sent one at a time but does not say the feedback must be separate. The Lesson has two parts, so part 1 is not the last part and no wrap-up role-play was offered.

10. Benchmark runner vs skill on the script.
   The runner asks for the `audio-script` block as the clip stand-in, while the skill says to keep the script out of the message. I treated the block as the clip itself and showed the script again with pinyin and meaning only after the student answered.
