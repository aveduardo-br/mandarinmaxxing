# Helping out

mandarinmaxxing gets better when the people who study with it say what felt off. You don't need to code, or to be good at Mandarin, to help.

## Ways to help

- **A word in the wrong place.** If a word doesn't belong in its situation, or two words that look alike landed in the same lesson, say so.
- **A lesson that felt off.** Too long, too easy, a sentence nobody would say, a word that never came back.
- **A situation that's missing.** Something you needed to say and couldn't find.
- **A fix.** If you'd rather change the map or the text yourself, send a pull request.

Everything starts with [an issue](https://github.com/aveduardo-br/mandarinmaxxing/issues). Write in any language.

## How this project works

mandarinmaxxing is a community project with an owner, Gautech. It's worth being clear about what that means:

- **The skill is free, and it stays free.** That is written into the [license](LICENSE).
- **The maps and the lesson texts belong to the project.** Anyone can study with them and help improve them. Nobody can take them to build another product, or feed them to an AI to mass-produce copies. That protection is the reason the license exists.
- **What you contribute becomes part of the project.** You remain the author of what you send, you're credited in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md), and you give the project the right to use it, here and in anything Gautech builds from it later.
- **The method is free for anyone.** If you want to build your own course by situation, go ahead. Just start from your own words.

If any of this doesn't sit right with you, say so in an issue before contributing. Better to talk first.
