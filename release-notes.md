The same HSK words, reorganized around everyday conversations.

**What's new**

- **A setup screen.** A new course opens with a short card: choose your level, pace and audio with a click. Type `setup` in any chat of your course to see where you are, continue, review, jump to a lesson, or change your choices.
- **Stroke order in the vocabulary table.** Point at a word and its characters draw themselves, stroke by stroke.
- **A lighter, steadier skill.** The instructions every lesson reads are about 40% shorter. Each part is now planned whole before it starts and checked by script, so lessons use only words you've met and vary their sentences more.
- **A longer first session.** HSK 1 opens with all of lesson 1 in one sitting, so you get real exchanges from the start. HSK 1 is now 57 parts at the recommended pace.
- **Honest pace names.** Light is 3 or 4 new words a day, recommended is 5 to 7, intense is 7 to 10.
- **HSK 5 and 6** are still built when you start them, and the map is now checked by script against the official list.

Your `course.md` from 0.5 keeps working.
