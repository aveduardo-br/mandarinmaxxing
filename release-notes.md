The same HSK words, reorganized around everyday conversations.

**What's new**

- Lessons are everyday situations with fixed numbers, studied in short parts at your pace (3, 5, 7, or any number).
- A hand-checked map for HSK 1: 300 words in 22 lessons, starting with a seven-lesson jump start.
- The official November 2025 word lists for HSK 1 to 6 are bundled.
- Your course is saved in your Claude project, so it follows you to any device.
- New vocabulary table with a handwriting-style Hanzi font that loads by itself.
- Clearer corrections: what you wrote, what to change, and the corrected sentence.
- Optional audio: one short clip per part in a natural voice, through a voice provider such as ElevenLabs.
- Simpler setup message and install steps.

**Install**

Download `mandarinmaxxing-claude.zip` below and follow the steps in the README.
