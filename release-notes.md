The same HSK words, reorganized around everyday conversations.

**What's new**

Fixes from the first benchmark of 0.5.0 (nine simulated sessions, results in `qa/benchmark/results/round-1/`):

- The first parts of a course, when you know only a handful of words, now have two rounds instead of three, with short exchanges in a real order instead of shuffled greetings.
- Dictation sentences are one person's line each, never both sides of an exchange.
- Every part ends with an offer to start the next one.
- The review keeps the total it announces, and a short previous part is filled with older words instead of stretched sentences.
- Older words come back one per earlier line, picked before the review, so they actually show up.
- Words you missed stay on your list only if you didn't get them right later in the same part, and the list holds words and short phrases, not whole sentences.
- Prompts are always the line itself: no stage directions, no "he said, I replied". Polite forms like 您 are cued the way you'd say them ("Bom dia, senhor!").
- Dictation, audio and round names are saved in your course, so every chat uses the same words.
