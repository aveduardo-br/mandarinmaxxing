The same HSK words, reorganized around everyday conversations.

**What's new**

- In the first parts of a course, when you know only a handful of words, you get fewer exercises instead of the same greetings repeated or shuffled. The dictation has two or three real lines instead of doubled words.
- To ask you for the polite 您, the sentence uses "senhor" or "senhora" where 您 goes ("Obrigado, senhor!"), not as a call at the start that the answer doesn't translate.
