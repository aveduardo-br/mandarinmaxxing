Source: HSK syllabus released 2025-11 (200 words). Each situation is one lesson, numbered as below: 19 lessons. Parts at paces 3 / 5 / 7: 66 / 38 / 29. Lessons 01–04 are the jump start. Under each lesson, the `Split` line gives the number of blocks in each part at paces 3 / 5 / 7, and each `- ` line is one block, in teaching order. Entries keep the official spelling, including sense numbers such as 别1. The list has 过 twice: the particle guo in lesson 03 and the verb guò in lesson 09.
