# HSK 4 map

Source: HSK syllabus released 2025-11 (1000 words). Each situation is one lesson, numbered as below: 55 lessons. Parts at paces 3 / 5 / 7: 317 / 172 / 130. Lessons 01–05 are the jump start. Under each lesson, the `Split` line gives the number of blocks in each part at paces 3 / 5 / 7, and each `- ` line is one block, in teaching order. Entries keep the official spelling, including sense numbers such as 生1. The list has 重 twice (zhòng, heavy, in lesson 50, and chóng, again, in lesson 51) and 空 twice (kòng, free time, in lesson 11, and kōng, empty, in lesson 26).
