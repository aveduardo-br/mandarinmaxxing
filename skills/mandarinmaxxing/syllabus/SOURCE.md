# Word lists

Vocabulary for HSK levels 1–6 from the HSK syllabus (《HSK 考试大纲》) released in November 2025 by the Center for Language Education and Cooperation, in force from July 2026.

- Official query system: https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus
- These files were converted from the CSV mirror at https://github.com/ofou/hsk (MIT License), which is checked against the official table and the published PDF.

Columns: official number, word, pinyin, part of speech, and level as printed in the syllabus. A level such as `一级（四级）` means the word returns at a higher level with another sense. All rights in the syllabus stay with its publisher.
