# Levels: finishing one, and HSK 5 and 6

Read this when the learner finishes a level, when `course.md` says `Next: level complete`, or when they ask for HSK 5 or 6.

## When a level ends

A level ends when every lesson on the route has all its parts in the **Done** log. After that last part, save as usual with `Next: level complete`. In place of the usual closing line, congratulate the learner in one line and offer the next level. After HSK 4, see "HSK 5 and 6" below.

A later chat that finds `Next: level complete` offers the next level again. If the learner declines, leave the file as it is: reviews and extra practice still work.

If they accept:

1. Read the new level's map.
2. Keep the same `course.md`. Add the finished level's prefix to every **Done** line that has none. Update the level in **Syllabus**, replace **Route** and the **Lessons** list with the new level's, and set **Next** to part 1 of its first lesson. Keep everything else, including the whole **Done** log, so words from the finished level keep coming back.
3. Show the new level's overview as in `setup.md`, leaving out the sentence about lessons, parts and pace, and ask about the route. Save again if it changes.
4. Ask whether to start the first part now or next time, and start only if they say now.

If the learner later reviews a lesson of a finished level, take its name from that level's map.

## HSK 5 and 6

The course covers HSK 1 to 4. Levels 5 and 6 have a word list in `syllabus/` and no map yet, and a map is never built in the chat: the maps of this course are written and checked before anyone studies with them.

- A learner who asks to start at HSK 5 or 6: say in one line that those levels aren't mapped yet, and offer HSK 4, from the lesson they choose.
- A learner who finishes HSK 4: congratulate them, say that HSK 5 will come in a later version of the skill, and leave `Next: level complete`. Reviews, role-plays, extra readings and flashcards still work.
